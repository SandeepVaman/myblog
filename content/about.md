I’m Sandeep. Build Log is where I work through the machinery underneath databases and distributed systems: pages, trees, transactions, storage layouts, failure modes, and the trade-offs hidden behind simple APIs.

The articles are written as practical explanations, grounded in real implementations. The current series studies BoltDB piece by piece and follows that understanding into a Rust implementation.

You can find my work on [GitHub](https://github.com/SandeepVaman) and connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sandeep-vb-335b8097/).

If you enjoy careful explanations of boring, dependable technology, you’re in the right place.
